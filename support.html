Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. LitterBook has no account and no cloud. To move to a new phone, open Settings → Export backup, save the file (for example to Files or iCloud Drive), then use Settings → Import backup on the new phone.

How is the due date estimated?

If you mark an estimated ovulation day on the heat (from progesterone results), LitterBook counts 63 days from it. Otherwise it shows a 58–68 day window from the first breeding date. These are estimates only. Your veterinarian's guidance comes first.

How do weight alerts work?

After each weigh-in, a puppy is flagged if it weighs less than at its previous weigh-in, or if it gained less than your chosen percentage over about 24 hours (Settings → Weight alert). It's a reminder to take a closer look, not medical advice.

Will Whelping mode keep running if I lock my phone?

The screen stays awake while Whelping mode is open. If you leave the app, your session and timers are saved. Reopen LitterBook and tap "Whelping in progress" on Home. If you allowed notifications, the long-gap reminder still arrives.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → LitterBook and make sure notifications are allowed. Task reminders arrive at 8:00 AM on the day they're due.

Can I try it with example data?

Yes. Settings → Load sample data adds an example kennel. When you're ready to start fresh, use Settings → Reset all data.

Is there a subscription?

No. LitterBook is a one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads, no account.
