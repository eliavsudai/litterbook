LitterBook

Privacy Policy

Short version: LitterBook collects nothing. Your records live on your iPhone.

Data Not Collected. LitterBook has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never see your dogs, your litters or your photos.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone. That includes dogs, heat cycles, progesterone results, breedings, litters, puppies, weights, tasks, notes, settings and photos. It is never uploaded anywhere by LitterBook.

Photos

Photos you take or choose are copied into LitterBook's own storage on your phone. They never leave the phone unless you share them yourself, for example as a puppy card, a PDF or a backup file.

Sharing, on your terms

Puppy cards, PDF reports and backup files are created on your phone. They go only where you send them using the iOS share sheet: Messages, Mail, Files, AirDrop, or whatever you choose. After that, the app you shared with handles the file under its own privacy terms.

Permissions

  • Camera and Photos: requested only when you add a photo, and used only for that photo.
  • Notifications: requested only when you set a reminder. All reminders are scheduled locally on your phone.

Backups

"Export backup" creates one file containing your records and photos, and you decide where to save it. If you store it in iCloud Drive or email it, Apple's or your email provider's terms apply to that copy.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → "Reset all data" in the app, or delete the app. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

LitterBook does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date below. LitterBook will never start collecting data without asking you first.
